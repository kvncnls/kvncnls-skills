# About Compass

Compass is a product-judgement Skill for designing and auditing how people move across screens and states without becoming lost, trapped, or forced to repeat work.

Its north star is **Never Lost**. A finite journey has an entry and outcome. An open-ended journey has an intent and stable home anchor; it does not need a fabricated finish line or progress meter.

Three disciplines support the method:

1. **Orientation**—position, meaningful direction, and a platform-appropriate way to retreat, return home, or exit.
2. **Path Economy**—the fewest honest steps for the intent, without removing protection or hiding consequence.
3. **Continuity**—context and state survive transitions, interruption, resumption, and alternate entry points.

Compass can build a Flow Spec or audit an existing journey. Audits score the three disciplines `0–4` for a native `/12`, explain every score from evidence, and return up to three warranted top moves with exact Screen · Flow · State · Lifecycle locators. A `3/4` is strong professional work; a `4/4` is above-and-beyond and intentionally uncommon.

Compass owns the path and its seams. Focal owns the screen, Flywheel the relationship, and Soul authored memory. It does not require a literal Back control on every screen or treat every optional step as waste.
